## What changed

- **`packages/llm`** (new, `@name-atlas/llm`): the model client. `completeJson` posts a `developer` and a `user` message to OpenAI's Chat Completions API over plain `fetch` with a strict JSON-schema `response_format` and `max_completion_tokens`, aborts after a timeout, and reads the object at `choices[0].message.content`; a refusal, a `finish_reason` of `length`, a non-JSON or non-object answer, an error status, a failed request and a missing key are each an `LlmError` of its kind. The key is sent in one header and is in no error, log or row. No dependency. Checked against the API reference on 2026-10-06 (the reference recommends the Responses API; Chat Completions is not deprecated and its `json_schema` format is documented).
- **Migration 0047** (`packages/db/migrations/0045_given_name_readings.ts`): `given_name_readings`, one row per unordered pair of names (`given_name_a_id <= given_name_b_id` as text, a CHECK), the two claims read, the prompt version, the model, both texts and the time; unique on the pair, the claims and the prompt; a trigger refuses `UPDATE` and `DELETE`; `down` refuses while a reading is stored. `packages/db/src/generated/db.ts` regenerated with `pnpm db:generate-types`; `migrator.test.ts` lists 0047.
- **`packages/truth/src/given-name-readings.ts`** (new): `readingKey` (the pair ordered, each claim with its name), `findReading`, `storeReading` (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, then the stored row), `countReadingsSince`. Exported from the package.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/given-name-meaning-view.tsx`** (new) and **`report-table.tsx`** (new): the full-name report's first-name meaning block (`GivenNameMeaning`, `ORIGINS`, the words) and its `Table` moved out of `report-view.tsx` and exported, so the report, the baby-names page and the couple page render one component. The report's output is unchanged (its tests pass as they were).
- **The couple page**, `apps/web/src/lib/couple-names-*.ts(x)`, `apps/web/src/app/pl/imiona-pary/`, `en/couple-names/`, `api/couple-names/`:
  - `couple-names-config.ts`: `COUPLE_NAMES_ENABLED`, the paths, `private, no-store`, the fields, the status words, `COUPLE_NAMES_DAILY_CAP = 200`, `COUPLE_NAMES_MODEL = "gpt-6-luna"`, `COUPLE_NAMES_PROMPT_VERSION = "couple_names_reading@1"`, `COUPLE_NAMES_BANNED` (TASK-100 §3.4's words as patterns, plus "idealn", "stworzeni dla siebie", "procent", "%", "perfect", "score", "percent", hearts; `zgodn(?!ie)` keeps "zgodnie z"), and `checkReading`: no banned pattern, no digit, 80–260 words.
  - `couple-names-reading.ts`: `whyNoReading` (no source, rights blocked, or an origin only: a reading needs a quoted meaning on both sides), `readingInput` (the two claims' entries as JSON: author, title, origin as printed and in the project's words, meaning, translation; no locator, year, surname, account or typed text), `READING_INSTRUCTIONS` (ADR-0047 §2.3's rules), `READING_SCHEMA`, `parseReadingOutput`, `readingParagraphs`.
  - `couple-names-page.tsx` and `couple-names-view.tsx`: signed out, the explanation and sign-in (the page is a return address, `safeNext`); signed in, two fields; each found name's meaning block from its claim on the FREE surface with its citations; then the reading block: `STORED`, `OFFER` (the button), `NEEDS` (which name and why), `UNAVAILABLE` (no key). The same name twice is shown once. `noindex`; the middleware sends `private, no-store`.
  - `couple-names-routes.ts`: `POST /api/couple-names`. Same-site, signed-in, form only; resolves both names, reads both meanings, goes back to the page in every case; calls the model only for two quoted meanings with no stored reading, below the day's cap and with a key; at most two calls; each language's text checked; a passing text stored once per pair; `?reading=failed|limit|unavailable` otherwise. Logs ids, kinds, token counts and durations only.
  - `couple-names-runtime.ts`: the flag, the accounts service and the key read at call time; the page's and the route's deps over `db()`.

- `pnpm test:integration` as a whole, `pnpm test:golden` and `pnpm test:e2e` were not run; only the new integration file was.
- On production and staging the web app reads `public` through a read-only role: **it cannot insert a reading until that role is granted `INSERT` on `given_name_readings`** (`ops/vm`, the grant step of the next VM change). The flag stays unset until migration 0047 is applied there, the first-name meanings are imported (TASK-147's `given-names` step, not yet run on the VM) and the grant exists.
