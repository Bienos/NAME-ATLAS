## What changed

- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-share-facts.ts`** (new, pure): `babyFreeCardFacts` and `babyPaidCardFacts` turn one sex's page facts (`BirthNameFacts` from `readBirthNameFacts`, `GivenNameFacts` from `readGivenNameFacts` with `stockListOfSex`) into what a card says, every figure through `formatInteger`, `formatPercent`, `formatDecimal`, `formatDate`. The free card: the latest year's count, "Nr N" with "z M imion na liście", "1 na N" with the dot count when N ≤ `DOT_GRID_MAX` and the page's caption, the direction word with "4,0% → 3,7% · 2020→2025" ("poniżej progu →" when `earlier_share_per_million` is null), the register's "Dziś N kobiet/mężczyzn w Polsce ma to imię" and "R. miejsce z M", the source line with the list's year and the register's date, the privacy line, the host. Null when the sex has no count on its latest list; an absent rank, share, direction or register leaves its element out. A share that would read 0,0% at one decimal takes two or three (`formatShare`; MYROSLAVA's 275 per million reads "0,03%"). The paid card: one entry a year from `history` (`bar` with its height as a share of the peak, `cutoff` for a stored list the name is not on, `gap` for no list; roles first/peak/latest with the page's `peakOf`), "×N" only when 2000's and the latest counts both exist (the inverse with "rzadziej"), the three callouts ("2017 · najwięcej · 3. miejsce", "—" without a rank claim), the ladder from the page's `ladderYears`, "Numer 1 w 2016, 2017 i 2025 r.", the register sentence, the source line with "listy 2000–2025". Null for a sex with no count in any year or without the history. Also the card keys (`story`, `square`, `paid`) and sizes, `babyCardPath`, `babySharePath`, `babyPagePath`, `babyCardFileName` (`zofia-girls-story.png`, through `fileStem`), `parseBabyShareQuery`.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-share-card.tsx`** (new): the three faces with `next/og`, after the canvas's boards. The tokens by value with their semantic names in comments (`atlas-plum-850` ground, `atlas-plum-700` tile, ivory 50/200/300 text, `dusk-line`/`dusk-line-control` borders, `atlas-blue-500` for the lit dot and the ladder's latest point, `atlas-blue-300` for the big figure and the address, `atlas-amber-500` for the peak bar alone, `atlas-aubergine-500` for the other bars), the fonts of `og-card.tsx` (`loadFonts`), the Evolved Contour mark's five strokes and point inline. The name sized to fit one line (`babyNameSize`), a tile's figure sized to its line, the dot grid nine to thirteen a row on the story card (`dotMetrics`) and five to eight beside the name on the square card. `renderBabyCard(facts, card, headers)`. The frame is a component, not a fragment: `next/og` lays a fragment's children out in one row.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-share-route.ts`** (new) and the route files `apps/web/src/app/pl/imiona-dla-dziecka/karta/[card]/route.ts`, `apps/web/src/app/en/baby-names/card/[card]/route.ts`: `babyCardResponse` answers 404 for an unknown card, without a session, without a sex in the query, for the paid card without `hasFullView` (checked before `findName`, so an unentitled request reads no claim), for a name not FOUND and for a sex without a card; otherwise the PNG with `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. `babyCardRoute` adds the flag and the accounts runtime (404 when off), the session cookie (`readBabyNamesSession`) and the page's deps (`runningBabyNamesDeps`). The host comes from `requestOrigin`.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-share-page.tsx`** (new) and `apps/web/src/app/pl/imiona-dla-dziecka/udostepnij/page.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/en/baby-names/share/page.tsx`: the sheet. `loadBabyShare`: signed out → the sign-in link with `next` = the page (the page's convention); no name or sex → one line and a link to the page; a name not found or without a free card for the sex → "Dla tej płci nie ma karty" and the way back; otherwise the sheet for the name, sex, choice (`?card=basic|paid`, the paid one only when entitled and the paid card exists, else basic) and format (`?format=story|square`; the paid card is story-only). `BabyShareView` in `<main class="baby-names atlas-v1 bn-share-page">`: the picker (links with `aria-current`; the paid option a `<span aria-disabled>` reading "8,99 zł · Wkrótce" without the entitlement), the preview `<img width height alt>` of the card route, the format toggle (links) or the story-only note, the island, the link note, the footer line (free or paid wording). `noindex`.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-share-client.tsx`** (new, the island): "Udostępnij" (`shareOrSaveImage`: the share sheet with the file where `canShareFiles()`, a download elsewhere), "Zapisz obraz" (an `<a download>` link, which works without script; a button through the share sheet on iPhone and iPad, `savesThroughSheet`), "Kopiuj link" (`navigator.clipboard.writeText` of the server-supplied page address; on refusal the address is shown to copy by hand), one `role="status"` line.
