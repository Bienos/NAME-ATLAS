# 2026-10-04 — TASK-094: the family tree's invitations and claims

**Task:** `tasks/094-invitations-and-claims.md`. **Number:** 094, the next free on `main` (up to 093) with no open pull request. **Requested by:** the project owner, "Go" (2026-10-04), after the Mapa (TASK-092, #100); STATUS named invitations and claims as the tree's next step. **Specification:** ADR-0029 §2.6.20–23 and §2.3.10; D-031 (a relative who declines contact stays as the owner's private row, pending legal review).

## What changed

- **Migration 0033** (`packages/db/migrations/0033_family_invitations.ts`): `family.persons` gain `claimed_by_user_id` (one account per row; a partial unique index makes it one row per tree per account), `claimed_at`, `contact_declined` and `hidden_fields` (a `text[]` held to `birth_year`, `birth_place`, `birth_surname`, and to a claimed row); `family.invitations` (tree, person, the token's and the address's SHA-256 as hex, sent by, sent at, expiry, the answer and who gave it; one pending per person); `family.suppressions` (tree, address hash); the audit kinds `INVITATION_SENT`, `PERSON_CLAIMED`, `CLAIM_RELEASED`, `CONTACT_DECLINED`. The policies: `persons_select` lets a claimant read their own row and a pending invitation's holder (the token's hash in the transaction as `app.invitation`) the same, both with the parents' names through `own_card_visible`, and hides a declined row from everyone but the owner (`person_visible` too, so `parentage` follows); `persons_update` refuses an owner or editor on a claimed row and lets the claimant update it; `parentage_select` shows a claimant their own links; `audit_insert` takes a claimant's event; `invitations` are the owner's to read and insert, deletable while unanswered, readable by the token's holder. `family.answer_invitation` and `family.release_claim` (SECURITY DEFINER) are the only way a claim changes. The down refuses while a claim is stored.
- **The service** (`packages/family/src/service.ts`, `rules.ts`, `schema.ts`): `invite` (owner only, under the tree lock; `inviteProblem` for `NOT_LIVING`, `MINOR`, `CLAIMED`, `DECLINED`; then `SUPPRESSED`, `PENDING`, `LIMIT` of ten per 30 days; an expired unanswered row gives way), `withdrawInvitation`, `invitationOf`, `openInvitation`, `answerInvitation`, `listCards`, `getCard`, `updateCard` (`checkPerson` on the shared fields as `LIVING`, `checkHiddenFields`), `leaveTree`; `getTree` carries `claimed`, `claimed_by_me`, `hidden_fields`, `contact_declined` and blanks a hidden field for everyone but the claimant; `updatePerson` answers `CLAIMED` for a row someone else holds. The token and the address reach the service only as hashes.
- **The contract** `tree` 1.0 → 1.1 (`docs/contracts/tree.md`, `packages/contracts/src/tree.ts`): the four fields, with a refinement that only a claimed row is the caller's or hides a field.
- **The web layer:** `tree-paths.ts` (pages `invitation` with the token in `t`, `cards`, `card`; the actions `invite`, `answer-invitation`, `update-card`, `leave-tree`; `isToken`, `invitationAnswer`, `draftOfCard`, `cardFromDraft`); `account-paths.ts` (`safeNext` and `isTreeNext` take the invitation page with a well-formed token, so the mail's link survives the sign-in page); `tree-mail.ts` (new: the invitation in both languages, naming the owner by the address of their account, with the link and no name, date or place); `tree-load.ts` (the three kinds, `cards` on the dashboard, the person's latest invitation with `pending` decided in the loader, the sign-out redirect back to the invitation); `tree-routes.ts` (`invite` makes the token with the accounts package, hands the service the hashes, mails the token, takes the row back when the mail fails; the other three; `mailer` and `origin` as new dependencies; the session's `email` for the mail); `tree-page.tsx` (`InviteSection`, `ClaimedRow` read-only with the hidden marks, `CardFacts`, `InvitationView` with the three answers, `CardsView`, `CardView` with the hide boxes and the way to leave; `PlaceFields` factored out of `PersonFields` for the card's form; the dashboard's link to the cards); `tree-text.ts` (the words and the status maps in both languages); `family-runtime.ts` and the API route (the mailer and the origin); `legal-tree.ts` (one paragraph on invitations in the notice's tree section, both languages; the terms clause and its date are unchanged); `globals.css`.
- **Tests:** `rules.test.ts` (the rules), `tree.test.ts` (the contract), `tree-paths.test.ts`, `account-paths.test.ts`, `tree-mail.test.ts` (new), `tree-load.test.ts`, `tree-routes.test.ts` (a fake mailer; a failing address), `tree-page.test.ts` (both languages; the page's own tag structure compared), `tests/integration/src/family-claims.test.ts` (new: the flow end to end as the app role, the policies with and without the token, each answer's trace, the limits, 0033's down), the migration lists in `migrator.test.ts`, `extensions.test.ts`, `canonical-key-v2.test.ts`.
- `docs/CODEMAP.md`, `STATUS.md`, `tasks/094-invitations-and-claims.md`.
