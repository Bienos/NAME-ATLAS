# 2026-10-01 — TASK-062: free accounts, the pages (dark by configuration)

Branch `claude/friendly-brown-6n5iwu`, built on `main` @ `fd6a871` (#64). Task file: `tasks/062-account-pages.md`. Decision: `docs/adr/0027-free-accounts.md`, amended here in §2.5, §2.9 and §5.

## Why

The project owner, after TASK-061 was merged: "can you do it". The library of TASK-061 had no page in front of it. This change is that web layer, and it is **off unless five environment variables are set**, so production does not change when it merges: no link, no page, no route, and the privacy notice and terms read as they did.

## What changed

- **The gate** (`accounts-config.ts`): accounts are on only when `ACCOUNTS_DATABASE_URL`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `ACCOUNTS_MAIL_FROM`, `SITE_ORIGIN` and `CRON_SECRET` are all set. It reads names and imports no package, so the header does not pull the database driver into every page. `accounts-runtime.ts` makes the one pool (two connections), the service and the Resend mailer, on first use.
- **The routes:** `POST /api/account/<action>` (sign-up, complete, sign-in, sign-out, reset-request, reset, delete) as functions over an injected service (`account-routes.ts`). Each checks the request came from this site, reads a small form, and answers a 303 to a page with a status word and nothing else; a mail goes out through `after()`, once the answer has gone. `GET /api/cron/accounts-purge` runs `purgeExpired` with `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET` (204 when accounts are off, 401 without the bearer, counts only in the answer); `apps/web/vercel.json` declares it daily.
- **The pages:** one catch-all route per language, `/pl/konto/[[...step]]` and `/en/account/[[...step]]`, for the account, sign-in, sign-up, reset, confirm and new-password steps. Plain HTML forms, no script. The sign-up form asks for an address and nothing else. The confirm page, opened by the mail's link, spends nothing on a GET; its form sets the password and takes the terms. All six are `noindex`; the two with a token in the address send no Referer to another site (`same-origin`).
- **The session cookie** (`session-cookie.ts`): `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Path=/`, and `Secure` with the `__Host-` prefix on HTTPS. Its lifetime is 400 days and the database row decides (ADR-0027 §2.5, amended).
- **The header** gains "Konto" / "Account" when accounts are on. The legal pages gain `legal-accounts.ts` when accounts are on: the notice stops saying there are no accounts and no cookies, and adds what an account holds, why, for how long, the cookie and Resend as a processor; the terms gain an account section. Both throw at build if a heading they change has been renamed, rather than publish text that no longer matches.
- **`middleware.ts`:** the account's forms get a bucket of their own, 10 a minute per client. The policies moved to `lib/request-policy.ts` so a test can read them.
- **Docs:** ADR-0027 amended (five variables; the 400-day cookie; the version of the terms recorded; the VM's dumps and migration under TASK-063); `.env.example`; `docs/CODEMAP.md`; D-026 (the product's name and domain); `STATUS.md`.

## Found by running it in a browser, not by the unit tests

1. **The same-site check refused a genuine form.** Next fills `request.url`'s host from the server's own configuration (`localhost`), not from what the browser typed (`127.0.0.1`), so the first version rejected every post made through another host name. The check now compares the `Origin` with `X-Forwarded-Host`, else `Host`, and uses `request.url` only as a last resort.
2. **`Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` on the two token pages made the browser write `Origin: null`** on their own same-origin forms, so the confirm and new-password forms were refused. The pages now say `same-origin` (another site still gets no Referer), and the check accepts a `null` Origin only when `Sec-Fetch-Site` is `same-origin`.
3. The terms line on the account page ended "r..", and the sign-out button sat against the heading below it.

A fourth was found in review and is covered by a unit test only: if the database is down, **sign-out still clears the cookie**.

## Not verified

- **The real Resend API.** A stand-in recorded the request; the provider's acceptance of it, a verified sending domain and the delivered mail's rendering in a mail client are untested. There is no Resend account yet.
- **Production.** The change deploys with the merge and none of the five variables is set there, so accounts stay off. That was checked on a local `next start` of the accounts-off build, not on the deployed site: see the last check under Verification. The migration is not applied in production (STATUS).
- **`apps/web/vercel.json`.** It is read only if `apps/web` is the Vercel project's root directory, which was inferred from `next` being a dependency of `apps/web` alone; the setting itself could not be read. Whether the cron is registered must be looked at under Settings → Cron Jobs.
- **`after()` on Vercel**, and **scrypt's wall time** (64 MiB per hash) on a Vercel function.
- **Whether Vercel's request logs keep the query string** of the two token pages. A token is single-use and lives 24 hours (sign-up) or one hour (reset), and Web Analytics drops the query (tested), but the platform's own log was not checked.
- **The privacy notice and terms are not a lawyer's review** (ADR-0023 §2.5). The Resend statements come from its own pages as a web search returned them (the Data Privacy Framework certification and the DPA's standard contractual clauses); the DPA itself was not read in full. The notice says nothing about backups and dumps: the VM's load scripts dump the whole database, and TASK-063 must exclude schema `accounts` from them or state their retention.
- **A distributed attack.** The per-client bucket is per instance and a speed bump; the per-address throttle in the database and the mail provider's own limits are the controls.
- **Assistive technology.** Labels, `role="alert"`/`status` and field attributes are in the markup and tested there; no screen reader was used.
