## What changed

- **`packages/truth/src/given-name-birth-facts.ts`:** `BirthListInfo.people_sum` (read from `given_name_birth_lists`); `birthShare(count, peopleSum)` gives a count claim its `share_per_million` (count × 1 000 000 ÷ the list's sum, rounded down, through `sharePerMillion` from `given-name-births.ts`, the trend claim's definition) and `one_in` (the sum ÷ the count, rounded); both null, never 0, when the stored figures contradict each other (a count above the sum, a sum below 1). `BirthCountFigure` carries them; `BirthTrendFigure` carries `latest_share_per_million` and `earlier_share_per_million` as the claim's `structured_value` states them (null when the claim lacks them or the earlier count was absent). `birthShare` is exported from `index.ts`. No new claim type; nothing is stored.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-page.tsx`:** the view rewritten in the `.atlas-v1` scope (`<main class="baby-names atlas-v1">`). New dependency `readMeaning(givenName, full)` (`readGivenNameMeaning`, PAID surface for an entitled reader, FREE otherwise); the sex focus from `?sex=boys|girls` (`BABY_NAMES_SEX_FIELD`), unasked the sex with a count on its latest list. Free, per sex: the hero tile (eyebrow, the count as the one big number, the noun, the share with one decimal, "Miejsce N" · "z M imion", the rank track with its marker at rank ÷ universe in Atlas Blue, the source line); "Jak częste" ("1 na N", a dot grid of N dots with the first lit when N ≤ `DOT_GRID_MAX` = 60, the caption); the direction (chip, "Udział: 2020 r. 4,0% → 2025 r. 3,7%", "poniżej progu" for an absent earlier count, the basis, the sources); the register tile (TASK-145's markup, unchanged facts). Then the meaning tile (each work's origin in the project's word with the work's words, the meaning verbatim, on the Polish page Name Atlas's translation marked "przekład Name Atlas" with the note, the work line in mono, the confidence badge; or one muted line "Brak danych o pochodzeniu i znaczeniu."; or the rights refusal) and the locked card (the same markup and words: "8,99 zł", "Wkrótce", no `<a>` or `<button>`). The sex tabs are `Tabs` with two in-page links carrying `aria-selected`/`aria-current`; the current sex's section comes first. Entitled: per sex an inline SVG (`YearChart`: one bar a year from `history`, heights from counts, the peak `bn-chart-bar-peak` in `--color-signal-amber`, the first and latest `bn-chart-bar-edge`, the rest `--chart-context`; a year with no count claim a dashed cut-off tick at the baseline, a year with no stored list a gap; axis labels for the first, peak and latest years, the peak's above its bar when within two years of an end), `TimesHeadline` ("×N" = latest ÷ 2000's count, or the inverse with "rzadziej", only when both exist), three callouts (`atlas-data-stat`), the ladder (2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020, the latest; "—" without a rank claim; the latest `aria-current`; the years at no. 1), the year table newest first with the last six open and the rest in `<details class="atlas-accordion">`, the sources numbered once; the other sex's one line when it is on no list in any year. `ORIGINS` in `report-view.tsx` is now exported for the origin words.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/baby-names-text.ts`:** the new copy in both languages (eyebrow, noun, share line, place, track labels, "1 na N" and its caption, the shares line, "poniżej progu", the meaning tile, the "×" wording, callouts, the chart's accessible name, the ladder, "Wszystkie N lat", the other sex's line).
- **`apps/web/src/app/globals.css`:** a block of rules scoped to `.baby-names.atlas-v1` (semantic tokens only; one column, two from 900 px; the form's submit in Atlas Blue with ivory text; links in the accessible blue; evidence lines in mono at 13 px).
