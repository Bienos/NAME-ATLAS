## What the register allows

TERYT gives a locality no coordinates (`places.centroid` is null for every SIMC place; the importer writes none), so the contract's `places` with `lat`/`lon` cannot be filled from it and a person cannot stand at a point. A locality does chain up through `administrative_memberships` to its voivodeship, whose external id (provider `TERYT`) is the two-digit TERC that `voivodeship-geometry.ts` keys the site's map of Poland by. The Mapa therefore places a person in their voivodeship, and says so in its lede and its note. The artboard's year slider is left for its own task.
