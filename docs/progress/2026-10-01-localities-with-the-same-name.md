# 2026-10-01 — TASK-066: localities with the same name as the surname, from TERYT, on the page and in the deck

Branch `claude/hopeful-bohr-05xd4b`, built on `main` @ `65ed574` (#68) and rebased onto `b48bd61` (#69). Task file: `tasks/066-localities-with-the-same-name.md`. Decision: ADR-0025 §2.7 (the owner's 2026-10-01 answer on the cards proposal: show localities with the same name under a warning line; each later card its own task).

## Why

The owner asked why not every piece of data in the cards proposal was linked to a card yet, then said "go ahead". Of what was left, localities with the same name was the one item already decided and unblocked: the TERYT register is loaded on the VM (TASK-039, TASK-041), `pl_teryt` has a stored rights profile, and the ADR names the task. Famous bearers (Wikidata) still needs a source registration and a decision about living people; the list-position and public-domain counts need wording and a contract decision; publication languages stay off the page. None of those is in this change.

## What changed

- **Contract `surname-result` 1.11.** A `localities` section, required and nullable, present exactly when `status` is `RESOLVED`. `READY` carries the register's reference date (`STAN_NA`), up to 20 whole localities in a fixed order (cities, villages, the rest; then voivodeship, powiat, gmina, name, id), the total, a confidence (`official_name_exact_key@locality_match@1`), the citation and the surface; `NOT_LOADED` (`TERYT_NOT_LOADED`) when the database holds no TERYT SIMC file; `RIGHTS_BLOCKED` on the source's rights. A section that is not `READY` carries none of the four. Zero matches is `READY` with no place. `docs/contracts/surname-result.md` records 1.10 → 1.11, rules 29–32 and the accepted rendering (the statement, then the warning line, then the list; never "comes from", "originates", "pochodzi", "lives in" or "mieszka").
- **Read path.** `packages/truth/src/localities.ts`: `pl_teryt`, its stored rights profile and its latest active SIMC file record in one query; the surface from that profile through `evaluateSurface`, as the archive section's comes from Polona's (`production_status` is read by the importer's gate only, as before); then the localities whose `place_names.normalized_name` is the surname's canonical key, with `spatial_precision = 'LOCALITY'`, joined through a membership to a `MUNICIPALITY` place (so a part of a locality, whose membership is to another locality, is never listed), then to the `COUNTY_DISTRICT` and the `REGION`, through the indexes migrations 0010 and 0023 made. The reference date is the matched places' `valid_from`, or, with no match, the `STAN_NA` the importer wrote into the file record's `universe_description`. The citation is the source row and the file record (title, publisher, the register's date as the publication date, `OFFICIAL_DATASET`, the file's description as the locator, the acquisition page as the url, the profile's attribution and terms, the retrieval time, the registry's homepage as the page to link). `query.ts` puts it on every resolved response, including the two paths where the register has no count. `version.ts` gains the two method constants; `index.ts` exports `localitiesSectionFor` and `TERYT_NOT_LOADED`. No claim, no derivation, no write, no migration.
- **The page.** `sections.ts` registers `places` (field `localities`, anchor `#places`, intent `places`) after the archive, so `sections.test.ts` still covers every contract section. `result-view.tsx` renders the chapter "Które miejscowości tak się nazywają?" / "Which localities have this name?" only when at least one locality matches: the statement (register, count, date), the warning line before the list, each locality with its kind (miasto / wieś / inna miejscowość, from TASK-039's `CITY` / `VILLAGE` / `OTHER`) and its gmina, powiat and voivodeship as the register writes them, and "i jeszcze N" where the list is shorter than the total. TERYT is listed once in the sources at the foot (ADR-0012), linked to `api.stat.gov.pl`, never to the download page; the notes carry the warning line and that only whole localities under their official name are listed.
- **The deck.** `deck.tsx` adds the `places` card after Polona's catalogue and before the summary: the count, up to five localities, "i jeszcze N", and the warning line on the card itself, so a screenshot keeps it; its source line is GUS and the register's date. It shares the summary image (TASK-056's rule for a card without an image of its own). `messages.ts` carries the strings in both languages and `localitiesSentence`, `localityKindLabel`, `localityAdminLine`, `localitiesNoun` and `localitiesMore`; `globals.css` the `.places` and `.dk-places` lists.
- **Tests.** `surname-result.test.ts` (contract rules and the 1.11 version); `deck.test.ts` (the card in its place, its figures on the page, the warning line, no origin words, no card without a match; the fixtures' sources count); `result-view.test.ts` (the chapter's statement, the note before the list, kinds and admin lines, the English, TERYT once in the sources with its link, no chapter for no match, `NOT_LOADED` or `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`; the journey's steps and the sources counts updated; the forbidden-words test now covers the chapter); every web fixture and the contract fixtures carry `localities`; `tests/integration/src/localities-with-name.test.ts` (below).
- **Docs.** `tasks/066-…`, `docs/contracts/surname-result.md`, `docs/CODEMAP.md`, `STATUS.md`, this record.
