## Why

The owner chose the first design (a dashboard, a consent step, the Konstelacja, Mapa and Rodowód views, a phone flow of one question per screen) and asked for the tree to be built. TASK-066 built what has no page in it; this is the first set of pages over it, the ones that need no script: the dashboard, the consent step, the Rodowód with its form, and the one-question flow. The Konstelacja and Mapa views, invitations and claims, export and matching are later tasks (the task file's §4).

Dark: the pages and routes exist only when accounts are on **and** `FAMILY_TREE_ENABLED=1`. Production sets neither, so nothing there changed.

## What changed

- **`apps/web/src/lib/family-config.ts`:** the gate, `familyTreeEnabled(env)`. **`family-runtime.ts`:** the service over the accounts pool, which `accounts-runtime.ts` now exposes (`pool`); `null` when off.
- **`tree-paths.ts`:** the five pages in both languages (`/pl/konto/drzewa/[[...rest]]`, `/en/account/trees/[[...rest]]`: the trees, `zgoda`/`consent`, `{tree}`, `{tree}/dodaj`/`add`, `{tree}/osoba/{person}`/`person/{person}`), the nine actions under `/api/tree/`, and the one-question flow's draft as a query (`draftFrom`, `draftQuery`, `personFromDraft`, `draftOf`): a reload of an intermediate page loses nothing but what is in its own address, and nothing is stored before the last POST.
- **`tree-routes.ts`:** the POST handlers over an injected service, as `account-routes.ts`: same site only, a session first (its user id is the only thing passed to the service), every answer a 303 with a status word, nothing a reader typed in a redirect to another page or in a log line. `create-tree` answers the new tree's first question; `add-person` saves, links to the child in the role (`setParent`), makes the first person of an empty tree the root, and answers the pedigree with the id; a problem goes back to the form with the draft kept; `update-person`, `delete-person` (with its confirmation), `set-parent`, `remove-parent`, `set-root`, `delete-tree`.
- **The living-people clause (AC-4):** a person marked living, or with no year of death, is saved only for an account whose `terms_version` is `TREE_LEGAL_UPDATED` (2026-10-03) or later; otherwise the answer is the consent page with the draft, and `accept-living-terms` records the version through the new `acceptTerms` of `@name-atlas/accounts` and then saves. A sign-up made while the tree is on records the tree variant's version (`accounts-runtime.ts`), so it never sees the step.
- **`tree-load.ts`:** the pages' decisions and reads, away from Next and React, so an integration test runs them as the real role: 404 when off or at an address that is not a page, sign-in without a session, a tree the reader may not open is 404 (ADR-0029 §2.3), a viewer is sent from the forms to the pedigree, the TERYT lookup only when the form asked for it.
- **`tree-pedigree.ts`:** the Ahnentafel slots (four generations; a hidden parent is neither empty nor the reader's to fill; nothing beyond an empty place), the next question breadth-first from the root (ADR-0029 §2.5.16), the people outside the pedigree, a person's parents.
- **`tree-places.ts`:** the birthplace lookup: a prefix match on `place_names.normalized_name` with the same `canonicalKey` the TERYT import wrote, SIMC localities only, with the district and the voivodeship up the `administrative_memberships` chain; on the read-only pool with no user id in the query (ADR-0029 §2.1.2). The chosen place's name is saved as `birth_place_text` beside the code, so the pedigree never reads the register.
- **`tree-page.tsx`, `tree-text.ts`:** the five pages in Polish and English, plain forms, no script, in the dusk direction: the dashboard (the trees as rows, a new-tree form, the next question, the privacy lines), the pedigree (cards, dashed empty places that link to "add this parent", the status chips, the hidden count for a member, the people outside the pedigree, the tree's deletion for its owner), the question (the choice of the missing parent, then the form of the design: names, birth surname, year with "około", a birthplace looked up in TERYT by a GET of the same page, living or not, a year of death, a source line, notes), the person's form (the same fields prefilled, the parents with their links, a link to an existing person, the root, deletion), and the consent step (the points, one checkbox, the draft as hidden fields, a way back with the draft). A viewer sees no form.
- **`legal-tree.ts`, `legal-text.ts`, `legal-page.tsx`:** the terms gain "Drzewo i osoby żyjące" / "The tree and living people" and the notice "Drzewo rodzinne" / "The family tree", applied on the accounts variant only when the tree is on, dated `TREE_LEGAL_UPDATED`; off, both documents read exactly as before (tested). **The text awaits the legal review D-031 already requires** before the tree goes live.
- **The header and the landing page:** "Drzewa" / "Trees" beside "Konto" when the tree is on (`site-nav.tsx`, `messages.ts`), leading to `tree-landing.tsx` at `/pl/drzewo-rodzinne` and `/en/family-tree` (added after the first build at the owner's request: the pages only after sign-in, and the header's link a page that explains the tree; redesigned the same day at the owner's "be bold" brief, after three artboard directions on the real tokens, of which the owner chose **Horyzont**). Public, static, no session, no form, no script. The hero shows before it explains: a sample pedigree of invented people (`SAMPLE_ROWS`, labelled "dane przykładowe · osoby wymyślone") drawn with the tree pages' own cards and chips, four rows receding to the horizon, the oldest smallest and dimmest with the sun behind it, "Ty" lit in front; each person appears in turn and the next question ("Kim była matka?") is lit at an empty place. Then: why (a surname is not a family; what a family remembers is lost within a generation; one question at a time; an empty place is a question), what the reader can do beside a phone frame holding the question form as a picture (read-only fields, no form element), who sees it (the same person blurred and "Osoba ukryta" for everyone, sharp with "Żyje · prywatne" for you), what is planned as five dashed cards (named as plans), and the two actions twice with the tier line. The motion is CSS alone: a timed sequence in the hero, a rise on entry for the sections where `animation-timeline: view()` is supported, nothing under `prefers-reduced-motion`. A 404 while the tree is off.
- **`apps/web/package.json`:** depends on `@name-atlas/family` (a workspace package, no new dependency). **`.env.example`:** `FAMILY_TREE_ENABLED`.
- **Tests:** `family-config.test.ts` (2), `tree-paths.test.ts` (7), `tree-pedigree.test.ts` (5), `tree-routes.test.ts` (14), `tree-load.test.ts` (7), `tree-page.test.ts` (8), `tree-landing.test.ts` (5), `legal-tree.test.ts` (5), `tree-nav.test.ts` (6), two cases in `analytics-path.test.ts`; `tests/integration/src/tree-pages.test.ts` (4): the routes and the loader over a real PostgreSQL as `nameatlas_app` with the roles file applied, the consent step recording the version, a member without `sees_living` reading the deceased and the count, the TERYT lookup as `nameatlas_web` over the excerpt.
