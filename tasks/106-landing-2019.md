# TASK-106 — The landing's Plate VII reads 2019–2026, as the surname page does

- **Status:** IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-04 (`docs/progress/2026-10-04-landing-2019.md`).
- **Requested by:** the project owner, 2026-10-04: "do the 2019 fix", after STATUS.md's "Change over time, open ends" listed it as the next step: "The landing's Plate VII reads 2020–2026 while the surname page reads 2019–2026; ADR-0019 §2.2 needs amending for 2019 there."
- **Depends on:** TASK-044 (Plate VII, `list_facts@3`), TASK-045 (the 2019 pair registered, ruling R-1), TASK-051 (ADR-0020: a surname's change reads 2019), all on `main`.
- **Decision:** ADR-0019 §5, an amendment, on the owner's ruling of 2026-09-30 (TASK-045 §2 R-1) and request of 2026-10-04.
- **Model guidance:** implementation session.

## 1. Goal

The site gives one first year for each list. Plate VII starts each list at its 2019-06-30 edition, the edition a surname's change over time already starts at, and says that the 2019 files describe their contents in fewer words.

## 2. Facts (checked on `main` @ `51c8c50`, 2026-10-04)

1. `PESEL_TREND_EDITIONS` (registry) is the 2019 edition of each list, then its series. A surname's change reads it. The list-facts generator and `fetch:pesel --series` read `PESEL_NATIONAL_SERIES`, which starts at 2020-01-22.
2. The 2019 descriptors have `series: null`, `header: "nazwisko,liczba"` and `universe_note_code: "UNIVERSE_DESCRIPTION_DIFFERS"`.
3. `list_facts@3` requires every edition's `series` to be the list's series.
4. The Plate VII footnote says the 2019 editions are left out.
5. Task number 106 is free on `main` and in open pull requests (104 is used by #119 and #120).

## 3. In scope

- `packages/contracts`: `list_facts@4`. An edition gains `universe_note_code`, and its `series` may be null, but only for the first edition and only with `UNIVERSE_DESCRIPTION_DIFFERS`. Tests, and `docs/contracts/list-facts.md`.
- `packages/truth`: `list_facts_pipeline@4` and `list_editions@2` (the first edition may be the 2019 one, parsed with its registry header), the generator reading `PESEL_TREND_EDITIONS`, the regenerated artefact, and tests.
- `packages/sources/bin/fetch-pesel.ts`: `--series` fetches the trend editions.
- `apps/web`: the Plate VII footnote in Polish and English, in the surname page's words. Test expectations follow the artefact.
- ADR-0019 §5 (amendment), an "Amended by" line in ADR-0020, and one line each in `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/CODEMAP.md` and `STATUS.md`.

## 4. Out of scope

- Any change to a surname's change over time, to the database, or to the registry's descriptors and their stored `coverage_notes`.
- The female 2022 gap, and `PRAWDA`'s 2020–2022 gap.

## 5. Acceptance

- **AC-1.** `pnpm truth:list-facts` over the byte-exact files writes an artefact whose `editions.items` per list equal `PESEL_TREND_EDITIONS`: 2019 first, with `series` null and the note. A second run is byte-identical.
- **AC-2.** The contract refuses an edition with no series anywhere but first, one without the note, and a note on an edition of the series.
- **AC-3.** The landing's Plate VII reads "… niż lista z 2019 r." and shows the 2019 bar and row, with no sideways scroll at 412 px.
