What open data can honestly say about a surname

Every figure on this page was computed from official open files: the PESEL surname lists for 2019–2026, the state register of geographic names, the SGJP grammatical dictionary and the US Census surname tables. Each one carries its source, its date and how sure we are. Where the data runs out, the page says so.
